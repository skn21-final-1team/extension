# Bookalpie Privacy Policy

**Last updated: March 17, 2026**

## Overview

Bookalpie is a Chrome extension that helps you manage Chrome bookmarks and sync them to Kalpie Notebook. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## Data Collection

### Data we access
- **Chrome Bookmarks**: Read and modify your bookmarks to provide bookmark management features (create, edit, delete, move, search).
- **Active Tab Information**: Access the current tab's URL and title only when you use the "Save Current Tab" or "Quick Save" feature.
- **Local Storage**: Store your preferences (theme, quick save folder, privacy consent status) locally on your device.

### Data we send to our server
- **Bookmark data**: When you explicitly choose to sync, selected bookmark folders and URLs are sent to the Kalpie Notebook server (`kalpie.net`) using your Sync Key.
- **Sync Key**: Used for authentication when syncing bookmarks to Kalpie Notebook.

### Data we do NOT collect
- Personal information (name, email, phone number)
- Browsing history
- Passwords or credentials (other than Sync Key for bookmark sync)
- Analytics or tracking data
- Data from websites you visit

## Data Storage

- All extension settings are stored locally on your device using Chrome's Storage API.
- Bookmark data is only transmitted when you explicitly initiate a sync to Kalpie Notebook.
- No data is collected, stored, or transmitted in the background.

## Third-Party Services

- **Google Favicon API**: Used to display website favicons next to bookmarks. Only the domain of bookmarked URLs is sent to Google's favicon service.
- **Kalpie Notebook** (`kalpie.net`): Bookmark data is sent only when you initiate a sync.

## User Control

- You choose which bookmarks to sync — only selected folders are transmitted.
- You can cancel sync at any time during transmission.
- Privacy consent is requested before the first sync.
- All local data can be cleared by removing the extension.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
